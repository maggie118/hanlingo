📝 HSK 1 Core Grammar 7 patterns

1. "是" (shì) — Subject + 是 + Noun

我是学生。
Wǒ shì xué sheng.
I am a student.
她是老师。
Tā shì lǎo shī.
She is a teacher.
这是书。
Zhè shì shū.
This is a book.

2. "有" (yǒu) — Subject + 有 + Object

我有朋友。
Wǒ yǒu péng you.
I have friends.
教室里有桌子。
Jiào shì lǐ yǒu zhuō zi.
There are desks in the classroom.
他没有钱。
Tā méi yǒu qián.
He has no money.

3. 正反疑问句 — Verb + 不 + Verb?

你好不好？
Nǐ hǎo bù hǎo?
Are you okay?
她是不是老师？
Tā shì bú shì lǎo shī?
Is she a teacher?
你去不去北京？
Nǐ qù bú qù Běi jīng?
Are you going to Beijing?

4. "在" (zài) — Subject + 在 + Place + Verb

我在学校学习。
Wǒ zài xué xiào xué xí.
I study at school.
妈妈在家做饭。
Mā ma zài jiā zuò fàn.
Mom is cooking at home.
我们在教室上课。
Wǒ men zài jiào shì shàng kè.
We are having class in the classroom.

5. 助动词 — 会 / 能 / 想 / 要

我会说中文。
Wǒ huì shuō Zhōng wén.
I can speak Chinese.
我想去中国。
Wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōng guó.
I want to go to China.
你要喝水吗？
Nǐ yào hē shuǐ ma?
Do you want to drink water?
他能来吗？
Tā néng lái ma?
Can he come?

6. "了" (le) — Completion or Change

我吃饭了。
Wǒ chī fàn le.
I have eaten.
她来了。
Tā lái le.
She has come.
天冷了。
Tiān lěng le.
It has become cold.
我到了。
Wǒ dào le.
I have arrived.

7. "把" (bǎ) — Basic Usage

请把书给我。
Qǐng bǎ shū gěi wǒ.
Please give me the book.
把门关上。
Bǎ mén guān shàng.
Close the door.
