Popular Business Chinese Sentences — HSK 4 to 9 Business Mandarin

Practice the real sentences used across Chinese business — from everyday workplace alignment to boardroom strategy and negotiation. These business Chinese examples span HSK 4 to 9, and pair every sentence with pinyin, a native English translation and the scene where it is used.

  • 我们需要对齐一下颗粒度。 Wǒmen xūyào duìqí yīxià kēlìdù. We need to align on the level of detail. Scene: cross-department alignment
  • 运筹帷幄之中，决胜千里之外。 Yùnchóu wéiwò zhī zhōng, juéshèng qiānlǐ zhī wài. Plan strategy in the command tent and win battles a thousand miles away. Scene: strategic planning
  • 擒贼先擒王。 Qín zéi xiān qín wáng. To win over the decision-maker first — the rest will follow. Scene: key-person strategy
  • 欲擒故纵。 Yù qín gù zòng. Loosen your grip so the other side reveals its real hand. Scene: negotiation psychology
  • 兼听则明，偏信则暗。 Jiān tīng zé míng, piān xìn zé àn. Listen to all sides and be enlightened; trust one side only and stay in the dark. Scene: boardroom decisions
  • 小不忍则乱大谋。 Xiǎo bù rěn zé luàn dà móu. If you cannot endure small things, you will ruin the grand plan. Scene: concession strategy
