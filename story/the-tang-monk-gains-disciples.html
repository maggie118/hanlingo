The Tang Monk Gains His Disciples

The Tang Monk frees the monkey from the mountain, and the monkey swears to protect him on the journey to the West. · HSK 1-2 · 3 sentences · pinyin + English

师父，快救救我！
Shīfu, kuài jiùjiu wǒ!
Master, please save me!
只要你救我出去，我保你西天取经。
Zhǐyào nǐ jiù wǒ chūqù, wǒ bǎo nǐ xītiān qǔjīng.
If you save me, I'll protect you on your journey to the West.
我孙悟空，愿意皈依佛门！
Wǒ Sūn Wùkōng, yuàn yì guī yī Fómén!
I, Sun Wukong, am willing to follow the Buddhist path!
