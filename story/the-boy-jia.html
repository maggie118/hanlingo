The Boy Jia

A brave ten-year-old boy discovers a fox demon possessing his mother and cleverly rallies the villagers to destroy it. · HSK 3-4 · 10 sentences · pinyin + English

楚客有贾于外者。妇独居，梦与人交。
Chǔ kè yǒu gǔ yú wài zhě. Fù dú jū, mèng yǔ rén jiāo.
A man of Chu traded far away from home. His wife, living alone, dreamed of intimacy.
有个十岁的孩子，叫贾儿，生性勇敢。
Yǒu gè shí suì de háizi, jiào Jiǎ Ér, shēngxìng yǒnggǎn.
There was a ten-year-old boy named Jia Er, brave by nature.
一天，他妈妈突然说胡话，行为很奇怪。
Yì tiān, tā māma tūrán shuō húhuà, xíngwéi hěn qíguài.
One day his mother suddenly talked nonsense and behaved strangely.
晚上，贾儿亲眼看见一只狐狸钻进了妈妈的房。
Wǎnshang, Jiǎ Ér qīn yǎn kànjiàn yì zhī húli zuān jìn le māma de fáng.
At night, Jia Er saw with his own eyes a fox slip into his mother's room.
原来那狐狸是妖怪，缠住了妈妈。
Yuánlái nà húli shì yāoguài, chánzhù le māma.
The fox was a demon that had possessed his mother.
贾儿不但不怕，反而定下了巧妙的计。
Jiǎ Ér bú dàn bú pà, fǎn'ér dìng xià le qiǎomiào de jì.
Far from being afraid, Jia Er quietly devised a clever plan.
他假装玩耍，暗中跟踪狐狸，发现了它的老巢。
Tā jiǎzhuāng wánshuǎ, ànzhōng gēnzōng húli, fāxiàn le tā de lǎocháo.
He pretended to play, secretly trailed the fox, and found its lair.
贾儿叫来乡亲，围攻老巢，把狐狸一举铲除。
Jiǎ Ér jiào lái xiāngqīn, wéi gōng lǎocháo, bǎ húli yìjǔ chǎn chú.
Jia Er rallied the villagers, surrounded the lair, and wiped out the fox.
妈妈醒来，身体一天天好转。
Māma xǐng lái, shēntǐ yī tiān tiān hǎo zhuǎn.
His mother awoke and recovered day by day.
大家都称赞贾儿：又勇敢，又机智。
Dàjiā dōu chēngzàn Jiǎ Ér: Yòu yǒnggǎn, yòu jīzhì.
Everyone praised Jia Er: brave and quick-witted.
