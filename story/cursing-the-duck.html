Cursing the Duck

A thief who steals his neighbor's duck grows feathers all over his itchy body until the wronged neighbor scolds him. · HSK 3-4 · 8 sentences · pinyin + English

汝病乃天罚，须得失者骂，毛乃可落。
Rǔ bìng nǎi tiān fá, xū dé shī zhě mà, máo nǎi kě luò.
A voice said: "Your illness is heaven's punishment. Only when the owner scolds you will the feathers fall."
有人偷了邻居家的鸭子，拿着回家煮来吃。
Yǒu rén tōu le línjū jiā de yāzi, názhe huí jiā zhǔ lái chī.
A man stole his neighbor's duck and cooked it at home.
第二天早上，他全身长满了鸭毛，痒得难受。
Dì-èr tiān zǎoshang, tā quánshēn zhǎng mǎn le yāmáo, yǎng dé nán shòu.
The next morning his body was covered in duck feathers and itched terribly.
他去请教一位老人，老人说：你得让人骂一顿，毛才会掉。
Tā qù qǐngjiào yí wèi lǎorén, lǎorén shuō: Nǐ déi ràng rén mà yí dùn, máo cái huì diào.
An old man told him: "You must be scolded once, and the feathers will fall off."
他不好意思承认，就骗邻居：你骂偷鸭人吧，我替你骂。
Tā bù hǎoyìsi chéngrèn, jiù piàn línjū: Nǐ mà tōu yā rén ba, wǒ tì nǐ mà.
Too ashamed to confess, he tricked his neighbor: "Scold the thief — I'll do it for you."
他边骂自己边偷偷欢喜，鸭毛果然一根根掉下。
Tā biān mà zìjǐ biān tōutōu huānxǐ, yāmáo guǒrán yì gēn gēn diào xià.
Scolding himself while secretly pleased, the feathers fell off one by one.
邻居觉得莫名其妙，他自己却虚出了一身冷汗。
Línjū juéde mòmíngqímiào, tā zìjǐ què xūchū le yì shēn lěng hàn.
The neighbor was baffled, while the man himself broke into a cold sweat.
从此，他再也不敢偷东西了。
Cóng cǐ, tā zài yě bù gǎn tōu dōngxi le.
From then on, he never dared steal again.
