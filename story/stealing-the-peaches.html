Stealing the Peaches

A street magician flings a rope straight into the sky and makes his son climb up to steal a fairy peach in a stunning illusion. · HSK 3-4 · 10 sentences · pinyin + English

童时赴郡试，值春节。
Tóng shí fù jùn shì, zhí chūn jié.
When I was a boy I went to the prefectural exam, and it happened to be New Year.
过年时分，街上人山人海，十分热闹。
Guònián shí fēn, jiē shang rén shān rén hǎi, shí fēn rènào.
At New Year, the streets were packed and lively.
一个变戏法的人大喊：我能上天偷仙桃！
Yí gè biàn xìfǎ de rén dàhǎn: Wǒ néng shàng tiān tōu xiāntáo!
A magician shouted, "I can climb to heaven and steal a fairy peach!"
他把一根长绳往天上一抛，绳子居然直直地立在半空。
Tā bǎ yì gēn cháng shéng wǎng tiān shang yì pāo, shéng zi jūrán zhí zhí de lì zài bàn kōng.
He flung a long rope into the sky, and it stood straight in midair.
他让儿子攀绳而上，爬进了云朵里。
Tā ràng érzi pān shéng ér shàng, pá jìn le yún duǒ lǐ.
He had his son climb the rope up into the clouds.
过了一会，一个大桃从天而落，大家争相喝彩。
Guò le yíhuìr, yí gè dà táo cóng tiān ér luò, dàjiā zhēngxiāng hècǎi.
Soon a big peach fell from the sky, and everyone cheered.
忽然，儿子的头也从天上掉了下来！
Hūrán, érzi de tóu yě cóng tiān shang diào le xiàlái!
Suddenly, the boy's head fell from the sky too!
变戏法人大哭：我儿子被神仙捉去了！人们连连捐钱安慰他。
Biàn xìfǎ rén dà kū: Wǒ érzi bèi shénxiān zhuō qù le! Rénmen liánlián juān qián ānwèi tā.
The magician wept, "The gods have taken my son!" People gave him money to console him.
结果，儿子却笑嘻嘻地从人群中钻了出来。
Jiéguǒ, érzi què xiàoxīxī de cóng rénqún zhōng zuān le chūlái.
Then the boy popped out of the crowd, grinning.
人们这才恍然：上当了！
Rénmen zhè cái huǎngrán: Shàngdàng le!
Only then did everyone realize: they'd been tricked!
