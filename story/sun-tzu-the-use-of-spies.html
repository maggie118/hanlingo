Sun Tzu: The Use of Spies

Victory belongs to those who know the enemy beforehand, and nothing replaces the timely intelligence that spies alone can provide. · HSK 5-6 · 1 sentence · pinyin + English

企业能领先同行，靠的是信息，明君贤将动而胜人者，先知也。
Qǐyè néng lǐngxiān tóngháng, kào de shì xìnxī, míng jūn xián jiàng dòng ér shèng rén zhě, xiānzhī yě.
Companies stay ahead of rivals through information — the wise succeed because they know the situation beforehand.
