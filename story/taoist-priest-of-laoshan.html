The Taoist Priest of Laoshan

A young man travels to Mount Lao to study Taoist magic, but learns the hard way that sincerity, not shortcuts, is the true way. · HSK 3-4 · 10 sentences · pinyin + English

少慕道，闻劳山多仙人，负笈往游。
Shào mù dào, wén láo shān duō xiānrén, fù jí wǎng yóu.
In his youth he longed for the Way; hearing that Mount Lao was full of immortals, he packed his satchel and went there to study.
有个叫王生的年轻人，一心想学长生之道。
Yǒu gè jiào Wáng Shēng de niánqīngrén, yīxīn xiǎng xué chángshēng zhī dào.
A young man named Wang Sheng was bent on learning the way to immortality.
他远赴崂山，拜一位老道士为师。
Tā yuǎnfù Láoshān, bài yí wèi lǎo dàoshi wéi shī.
He traveled far to Mount Lao and took an old Taoist as his master.
可是师父只让他砍柴打水，什么法术都不教。
Kěshì shīfu zhǐ ràng tā kǎn chái dǎ shuǐ, shénme fǎshù dōu bú jiāo.
But the master only had him chop wood and fetch water — no magic at all.
一天，客人用纸剪了个月亮，贴在墙上，四门就亮了。
Yì tiān, kèrén yòng zhǐ jiǎn le gè yuèliang, tiē zài qiángshang, sì mén jiù liàng le.
One day a guest cut a paper moon, stuck it on the wall, and the room lit up.
王生看得目瞪口呆，更加渴望学法术。
Wáng Shēng kàn dé mùdèngkǒudāi, gèngjiā kěwàng xué fǎshù.
Wang Sheng stared in awe and longed even more to learn magic.
师父告诉他：学法术，贵在心诚。
Shīfu gàosu tā: Xué fǎshù, guì zài xīn chéng.
The master told him: "Learning magic lies in sincerity of heart."
王生想：这么辛苦，还不如回家呢！
Wáng Shēng xiǎng: Zhème xīnkǔ, hái bù rú huí jiā ne!
Wang Sheng thought, "So much hardship — I'd rather go home!"
临走前，师父教他穿墙。他一头撞上墙，起了个大包。
Línzǒu qián, shīfu jiāo tā chuānqiáng. Tā yì tóu zhuàng shàng qiáng, qǐ le gè dà bāo.
Before he left, the master taught him to pass through walls. He ran headfirst into the wall and got a big bump.
这个故事告诉我们：世上没有投机取巧的本事。
Zhège gùshì gàosu wǒmen: Shì shang méiyǒu tōu jī qǔ qiǎo de běnshì.
This story tells us: there is no shortcut in life.
