Practical adult communication for everyday life: work, commuting, errands, health, social plans, and travel. Built for learners in China, Europe, the US, and Singapore. · HSK 3-4 · CEFR B1 · 12 sentences

我早上七点半起床，洗漱后按时去上班。
Wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn bàn qǐchuáng, xǐshuò hòu ànshí qù shàngbān.
I got up at 7:30 in the morning, washed up, and went to work on time.
我在地铁站等车，顺便看了一下手机里的邮件。
Wǒ zài dìtiě zhàn děng chē, shùnbiàn kàn le yíxià shǒujī lǐ de yóujiàn.
I was waiting for the subway at the station and checked my email on my phone.
你能不能帮我确认一下会议时间？我可能会晚一点。
Nǐ néng bu néng bāng wǒ quèrèn yíxià huìyì shíjiān? Wǒ kěnéng huì wǎn yìdiǎn.
Could you help me confirm the meeting time? I may be a little late.
我想请一天假，去医院看一下身体，不方便和大家说太多。
Wǒ xiǎng qǐng yì tiān jià, qù yīyuàn kàn yíxià shēntǐ, bù fāngbiàn hé dàjiā shuō tài duō.
I would like to take one day off to go to the hospital and check my health. It is inconvenient to explain too much to everyone.
