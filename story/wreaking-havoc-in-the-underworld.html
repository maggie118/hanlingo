Wreaking Havoc in the Underworld

The monkey storms the underworld and crosses his own name off the Book of Life and Death, declaring his fate his alone. · HSK 1-2 · 3 sentences · pinyin + English

生死簿在哪？我要划掉自己的名字！
Shēngsǐbù zài nǎ? Wǒ yào huà diào zìjǐ de míngzi!
Where is the Book of Life and Death? I'm crossing out my name!
从今天起，我命由我不由天！
Cóng jīntiān qǐ, wǒ mìng yóu wǒ bù yóu tiān!
From today on, my fate is in my hands, not heaven's!
这世界上，再也没有人能管我！
Zhè shìjiè shang, zài yě méi yǒu rén néng guǎn wǒ!
No one in this world can control me anymore!
