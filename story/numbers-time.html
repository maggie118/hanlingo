Numbers & Time

Tell the time, the date, and make plans — the practical numbers & calendar kit for everyday life. · HSK 1-3 · CEFR A1–A2 · 15 sentences

现在几点？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?
What time is it now?
现在三点。
Xiànzài sān diǎn.
It's three o'clock.
今天几号？
Jīntiān jǐ hào?
What's the date today?
今天星期几？
Jīntiān xīngqī jǐ?
What day is it today?
今天星期五。
Jīntiān xīngqīwǔ.
Today is Friday.
