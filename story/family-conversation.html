Family Conversation

Everyday family communication for adults: planning, caring, housework, and conversations at home. · HSK 3-4 · CEFR B1 · 4 sentences

今天晚上我来做饭，你负责收拾桌子和洗碗。
Jīntiān wǎnshang wǒ lái zuòfàn, nǐ fùzé shōushí zhuōzi hé xǐwǎn.
I will cook tonight; you are responsible for clearing the table and washing the dishes.
你今天工作太累了，晚上早点回家，别太熬夜。
Nǐ jīntiān gōngzuò tài lèi le, wǎnshang zǎo diǎn huíjiā, bié tài áo yè.
You worked too hard today; go home early tonight and don’t stay up too late.
