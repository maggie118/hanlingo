Planting Pears

A Taoist beggar magically grows a pear tree from a single core to teach a stingy pear seller a lesson in generosity. · HSK 3-4 · 10 sentences · pinyin + English

有乡人货梨于市，颇甘芳，价腾贵。
Yǒu xiāngrén huò lí yú shì, pō gān fāng, jià téng guì.
A countryman was selling pears in the market; they were sweet and fragrant, yet the price soared.
街上有个卖梨的人，梨卖得特别贵。
Jiēshang yǒu gè mài lí de rén, lǐ mài dé tèbié guì.
On the street there was a pear seller whose pears were extremely overpriced.
一个老道士路过，想要一个梨解渴。
Yí gè lǎo dàoshi lùguò, xiǎng yào yí gè lí jiě kě.
An old Taoist passed by and asked for a pear to quench his thirst.
卖梨人狠狠地说：不给，快走！
Mài lí rén hěn hěn de shuō: Bù gěi, kuài zǒu!
The seller snapped, "No! Get lost!"
道士说：你不给，我就自己种一个。
Dàoshi shuō: Nǐ bù gěi, wǒ jiù zìjǐ zhòng yí gè.
The Taoist said, "If you won't give one, I'll grow one myself."
他吃了一个梨，把梨核种到土里，不一会儿就长出一棵大树。
Tā chī le yí gè lí, bǎ líhé zhòng dào tǔ lǐ, bù yíhuìr jiù zhǎng chū yì kē dà shù.
He ate a pear, planted the core, and in no time a tall tree grew.
树上结满了香梨，道士摘下来分给路人吃。
Shùshang jiémǎn le xiāng lí, dàoshi zhāi xiàlái fēn gěi lùrén chī.
The tree bore sweet pears, which the Taoist picked and shared with everyone.
人们转头一看，那卖梨人的车早已空空如也。
Rénmen zhuǎn tóu yí kàn, nà mài lí rén de chē zǎo yǐ kōng kōng rú yě.
When they turned around, the seller's cart was already completely empty.
原来那棵树，是用他贵卖的梨变出来的。
Yuánlái nà kē shù, shì yòng tā guì mài de lí biàn chūlái de.
The tree had been made from the very pears he overcharged for.
他这才明白：人太吝啬，反而什么都留不住。
Tā zhè cái míngbái: Rén tài lìnxī, fǎn ér shénme dōu liú bú zhù.
Only then did he see: when you hoard too much, you end up with nothing.
