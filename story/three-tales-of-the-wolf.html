Three Tales of the Wolf

A butcher outwits two cunning wolves by seeing through their split-attack trick and killing both in an instant. · HSK 3-4 · 9 sentences · pinyin + English

狼亦黠矣，而顷刻两毙，禽兽之变诈几何哉？止增笑耳。
Láng yì xiá yǐ, ér qǐngkè liǎng bì, qínshòu zhī biànzhà jǐhé zāi? Zhǐ zēng xiào ěr.
The wolf was cunning, yet both were killed in an instant — how much trickery can beasts have? It only adds to the joke.
傍晚，一个屠户担着肉往家赶，半路遇见一只恶狼。
Bàngwǎn, yí gè túhù dān zhe ròu wǎng jiā gǎn, bàn lù yùjiàn yì zhī è láng.
At dusk, a butcher carrying meat met a fierce wolf on the road.
狼紧紧跟在后面，屠户跑多快，它就追多快。
Láng jǐnjǐn gēn zài hòumiàn, túhù pǎo duō kuài, tā jiù zhuī duō kuài.
The wolf kept close behind; the faster he ran, the faster it chased.
屠户躲到一个草垛后，放下担子，抽出屠刀。
Túhù duǒ dào yí gè cǎoduò hòu, fàng xià dānzi, chōu chū túdāo.
The butcher hid behind a haystack, put down his load, and drew his knife.
一只狼停下来，呆呆地望着他；另一只却悄悄绕到后面。
Yì zhī láng tíng xiàlái, dāi dāi de wàngzhe tā; lìng yì zhī què qiāoqiāo rào dào hòumiàn.
One wolf stopped and stared; the other quietly crept around behind.
原来那只狼在草垛后挖洞，想从后面偷袭。
Yuánlái nà zhī láng zài cǎoduò hòu wā dòng, xiǎng cóng hòumiàn tōuxí.
That wolf was digging a hole to sneak up from behind.
屠户看破了，乘其不备，回身连砍数刀，把两只狼都杀死。
Túhù kàn pò le, chéng qí bú bèi, huíshēn lián kǎn shù dāo, bǎ liǎng zhī láng dōu shā sǐ.
Seeing through the trick, the butcher turned and struck, killing both wolves.
原来前一只狼假装睡觉，只是为了诱敌。
Yuánlái qián yì zhī láng jiǎzhuāng shuìjiào, zhǐshì wèi le yòu dí.
The first wolf had only pretended to sleep, just to lure him.
蒲松龄感慨：人多疑，才能看破巧计。
Pú sōnglíng gǎnkǎi: Rén yào duō yì, cái néng kàn pò qiǎo jì.
Pu Songling reflects: one must stay alert to see through trickery.
