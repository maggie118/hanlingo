Office Daily Chat

The phrases you'll actually say every day in a Chinese office: acknowledge, align, review, reschedule and handle priorities. · HSK 4-6 · CEFR B1–B2 · 15 sentences

收到，我这边确认一下，稍后回复你。
Shōudào, wǒ zhèbiān quèrèn yíxià, shāohòu huífù nǐ.
Got it, let me confirm on my end and get back to you shortly.
这个需求我跟你对齐一下，你看什么时候方便？
Zhège xūqiú wǒ gēn nǐ duìqí yíxià, nǐ kàn shénme shíhou fāngbiàn?
Let me align with you on this requirement — when works for you?
我复盘了一下上周的项目，有几个问题想跟你讨论。
Wǒ fùpán le yíxià shàngzhōu de xiàngmù, yǒu jǐ ge wèntí xiǎng gēn nǐ tǎolùn.
I reviewed last week’s project — a few issues I’d like to discuss with you.
