Family & Friends

Talk about your family and friends — the heart of any warm conversation. · HSK 1-3 · CEFR A1–A2 · 15 sentences

这是我的家人。
Zhè shì wǒ de jiārén.
This is my family.
我家有四个人。
Wǒ jiā yǒu sì ge rén.
There are four people in my family.
你家里有几口人？
Nǐ jiā lǐ yǒu jǐ kǒu rén?
How many people are in your family?
我有哥哥和妹妹。
Wǒ yǒu gēge hé mèimei.
I have an older brother and a younger sister.
你爸爸妈妈做什么工作？
Nǐ bàba māma zuò shénme gōngzuò?
What do your parents do?
