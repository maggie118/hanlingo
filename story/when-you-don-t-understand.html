When You Don't Understand

Don't get stuck — ask them to slow down, repeat, write it down, or translate. Keep the conversation going. · HSK 1-3 · CEFR A1–A2 · 15 sentences

我听不懂。
Wǒ tīng bù dǒng.
I don't understand.
请说慢一点。
Qǐng shuō màn yìdiǎn.
Please speak more slowly.
请再说一遍。
Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.
Please say it again.
请写下来。
Qǐng xiě xiàlái.
Please write it down.
这个用中文怎么说？
Zhège yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō?
How do you say this in Chinese?
