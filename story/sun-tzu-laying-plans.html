Sun Tzu: Laying Plans

Sun Tzu's opening chapter: weigh every major decision carefully, because war is a vital matter of state and must never be taken lightly. · HSK 5-6 · 1 sentence · pinyin + English

做任何重大的决定，都要先想一想：兵者，国之大事，不可不慎重对待。
Zuò rènhé zhòngdà de juédìng, dōu yào xiān xiǎng yì xiǎng: Bīng zhě, guó zhī dàshì, bù kě bù shènzhòng duìdài.
Before any major decision, think it over: war is a vital matter of state and must be handled with care.
