Politeness & Asking for Help

Ask directions, say sorry, and get help — the polite phrases that make strangers want to help you. · HSK 1-3 · CEFR A1–A2 · 15 sentences

请问，洗手间在哪里？
Qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎlǐ?
Excuse me, where is the restroom?
请问，你知道这个地方吗？
Qǐngwèn, nǐ zhīdào zhège dìfang ma?
Excuse me, do you know this place?
对不起，我迟到了。
Duìbuqǐ, wǒ chídào le.
Sorry, I'm late.
没关系，我也刚到。
Méi guānxi, wǒ yě gāng dào.
It's okay, I just arrived too.
谢谢你的帮助。
Xièxie nǐ de bāngzhù.
Thanks for your help.
