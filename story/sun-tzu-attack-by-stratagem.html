Sun Tzu: Attack by Stratagem

The highest victory is subduing the enemy without fighting, and it comes from knowing both yourself and your rival thoroughly. · HSK 5-6 · 2 sentences · pinyin + English

做生意前先做市场调研，知彼知己，百战不殆。
Zuò shēngyi qián xiān zuò shìchǎng diàoyán, zhī bǐ zhī jǐ, bǎi zhàn bú dài.
Do market research before doing business — know your rival and yourself, and you'll never be in peril.
真正的强者不靠争吵取胜，不战而屈人之兵，才是上策。
Zhēnzhèng de qiángzhě bú kào zhēngchǎo qǔshèng, bú zhàn ér qū rén zhī bīng, cái shì shàngcè.
A truly strong person doesn't win through arguments — subduing the other side without fighting is the best strategy.
