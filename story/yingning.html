Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio · 聊斋志异 · 婴宁 · HSK 3-4

Yingning

A scholar falls in love with a sweet, endlessly laughing girl who turns out to be the daughter of a fox spirit. · HSK 3-4 · 9 sentences · pinyin + English

此女亦太憨。
Cǐ nǚ yì tài hān.
This girl is simply too guileless and sweet.
书生王子服在院子里遇见一位爱笑的姑娘。
Shūshēng Wáng Zǐfú zài yuànzi lǐ yùjiàn yí wèi ài xiào de nǚláng.
The scholar Wang Zifu met a laughing girl in the garden.
姑娘笑起来灿烂如花，叫婴宁。
Nǚláng xiào qǐlái cànlàn rú huā, jiào Yīng Níng.
She laughed as bright as a flower, and her name was Yingning.
王子服一见倾心，回家后居念不忘。
Wáng Zǐfú yí jiàn qīngxīn, huí jiā hòu jū niàn bú wàng.
Wang Zifu fell for her at first sight and could not stop thinking of her.
他到处寻访，终于找到了婴宁。
Tā dàochù xúnfǎng, zhōngyú zhǎodào le Yīng Níng.
He searched everywhere and finally found Yingning.
婴宁把他带回家，两人由母亲做主，结为夫妻。
Yīng Níng bǎ tā dài huí jiā, liǎng rén yóu mǔqīn zuòzhǔ, jié wéi fūqī.
She brought him home, and with her mother's blessing they married.
可谁也没想到，婴宁竟是狐仙之女。
Kě shuí yě méi xiǎngdào, Yīng Níng jìng shì húxiān zhī nǚ.
No one expected that Yingning was the daughter of a fox spirit.
无论受到怎样的屈辱，她总是以笑答之。
Wúlùn shòudào zěnyàng de qūrǔ, tā zǒngshì yǐ xiào dá zhī.
No matter how she was wronged, she always answered with a smile.
她的笑，让整个家充满了欢乐。
Tā de xiào, ràng zhěng gè jiā chōngmǎn le huānlè.
Her laughter filled the whole household with joy.
