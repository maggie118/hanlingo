Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio · 聊斋志异 · 娇娜 · HSK 3-4

Jiaona

A scholar risks his life to save the fox-spirit family who once cured him, and is saved in return by their devoted bond. · HSK 3-4 · 9 sentences · pinyin + English

余于孔生，不羡其得艳妻，而羡其得腻友也。
Yú yú kǒng shēng, bú xiàn qí dé yàn qī, ér xiàn qí dé nì yǒu yě.
I do not envy Scholar Kong his lovely wife, but his dear, devoted friend.
书生孔雪笠病入膏肓，无药可医。
Shūshēng Kǒng Xuělì bìng rù gāohuāng, wú yào kě yī.
The scholar Kong Xueli was gravely ill, beyond any cure.
朋友家的妹妹娇娜精于医术，亲手治好了他。
Péngyou jiā de mèimei Jiāonà jīng yú yīshù, qīn shǒu zhìhǎo le tā.
His friend's sister Jiaona, skilled in medicine, cured him with her own hands.
孔雪笠心生爱慕，可娇娜却要远嫁他乡。
Kǒng Xuělì xīnshēng àimù, kě Jiāonà què yào yuǎn jià tā xiāng.
Kong fell in love, but Jiaona was to be married far away.
后来，娇娜全家遭难，孔雪笠不顾生死，前去相救。
Hòulái, Jiāonà quán jiā zāo nàn, Kǒng Xuělì bùgù shēngsǐ, qián qù xiāngjiù.
Later, when Jiaona's family was in peril, Kong rushed to save them at the risk of his life.
在大祸中，他为救娇娜，自己却沉沉倒下。
Zài dà huò zhōng, tā wèi jiù Jiāonà, zìjǐ què chén chén dǎo xià.
In the catastrophe, he fell gravely wounded saving Jiaona.
娇娜反过来用医术救回了他的性命。
Jiāonà fǎn guòlái yòng yīshù jiù huí le tā de xìngmìng.
Jiaona, in turn, used her medicine to save his life.
原来他们一家是狐仙。这份生死之情，比亲情更深。
Yuánlái tā men yì jiā shì húxiān. Zhè fèn shēngsǐ zhī qíng, bǐ qīnqíng gèng shēn.
They turned out to be fox spirits. Their bond, forged in life and death, ran deeper than kinship.
正是：金石之交，超越人与狐。
Zhèng shì: Jīnshí zhī jiāo, chāo yuè rén yǔ hú.
Indeed: a bond of gold and stone, transcending human and fox.
