Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio · 聊斋志异 · 聂小倩 · HSK 3-4

Nie Xiaoqian

An upright scholar saves a beautiful ghost girl forced by a yaksha to harm people, and his kindness frees her to become his wife. · HSK 3-4 · 10 sentences · pinyin + English

妾阅人多矣，未有刚肠如君者。
Qiè yuè rén duō yǐ, wèi yǒu gāngcháng rú jūn zhě.
I have met many people, but never one with a heart as upright as yours.
书生宁采臣生性刚正，一心向学。
Shūshēng Níng Cǎichén shēngxìng gāngzhèng, yì xīn xiàng xué.
The scholar Ning Caichen was upright by nature and devoted to study.
一天，他借宿在一座古庙里，称为兰若。
Yì tiān, tā jiè sù zài yí zuò gǔ miào lǐ, chēng wéi Lánruò.
One night he lodged in an old temple called a lanruo.
夜半，一位娟美的女子敲门而入。
Yè bàn, yí wèi juànměi de nǚzǐ qiāo mén ér rù.
At midnight, a beautiful woman knocked and entered.
她说：我叫聂小倩，愿陪君夜读。
Tā shuō: Wǒ jiào Niè Xiǎoqiàn, yuàn péi jūn yè dú.
She said, "I am Nie Xiaoqian. Let me keep you company while you read."
宁采臣严词拒绝：我生平不亲女色。
Níng Cǎichén yán cí jùjué: Wǒ shēngpíng bù qīn nǚsè.
Ning refused sternly: "I have never been close to women."
小倩含泪说出实情：我是个鬼，被夜叉逼迫来害人。
Xiǎoqiàn hán lèi shuō chū shíqíng: Wǒ shì gè guǐ, bèi yèchā bī pò lái hàirén.
In tears, Xiaoqian confessed: "I am a ghost, forced by a yaksha to harm people."
宁采臣说：别怕，我来救你！
Níng Cǎichén shuō: Bié pà, wǒ lái jiù nǐ!
Ning said, "Don't be afraid — I'll save you!"
他以一把宝剑驱走了夜叉，小倩终于得以脱身。
Tā yǐ yì bǎ bǎojiàn qū zǒu le yèchā, xiǎoqiàn zhōngyú déyǐ tuōshēn.
With a magic sword he drove off the yaksha, and Xiaoqian was finally freed.
后来，小倩转为人间，成了宁采臣的妻子。
Hòulái, xiǎoqiàn zhuǎn wéi rén jiān, chéng le Níng Cǎichén de qīzi.
Later, Xiaoqian returned to the human world and became Ning's wife.
