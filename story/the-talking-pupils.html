The Talking Pupils

A frivolous man who leers at women goes blind, then regains his sight after a year of doing good deeds. · HSK 3-4 · 7 sentences · pinyin + English

长安士方栋，颇有才名，而佻脱不持仪节。
Cháng'ān shì fāng dòng, pō yǒu cái míng, ér tiāo tuō bù chí yí jié.
Fang Dong of Chang'an, a man of some talent, was frivolous and careless of propriety.
一天，他偷看一位经过的少妇，从此双目失明。
Yì tiān, tā tōukàn yí wèi jīng guò de shàofù, cóng cǐ shuāngmù shī míng.
One day he stared at a passing woman, and from then on went blind.
他后悔不已，去求菩萨保佑，愿以善还愿。
Tā hòuhuǐ bù yǐ, qù qiú púsà bǎoyòu, yuàn yǐ shàn hái yuàn.
Filled with regret, he prayed to the Bodhisattva, vowing to do good.
一天，他忽听眼睛里有人说话：若能行善，目自可复。
Yì tiān, tā hū tīng yǎnjing lǐ yǒu rén shuōhuà: Ruò néng xíng shàn, mù zì kě fù.
One day he heard voices in his eyes: "If you do good, your sight will return."
方栋便天天帮助别人，还抄录经书，送给穷人。
Fāng Dòng biàn tiāntiān bāngzhù biérén, hái chāo lù jīngshū, sòng gěi qióngrén.
Fang Dong helped others every day and copied scriptures for the poor.
过了一年，他的眼睛居然慢慢复明了。
Guò le yì nián, tā de yǎnjing jūrán mànmàn fùmíng le.
After a year, his eyesight gradually returned.
这个故事在说：知错能改，善有善报。
Zhège gùshì zài shuō: Zhī cuò néng gǎi, shàn yǒu shàn bào.
The story teaches: correct your faults and good will be rewarded.
