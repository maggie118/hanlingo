Hobbies & Sports

Talk about what you love — music, movies, reading, sports — the easiest conversation starter. · HSK 1-3 · CEFR A1–A2 · 15 sentences

你有什么爱好？
Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?
What are your hobbies?
我喜欢听音乐。
Wǒ xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè.
I like listening to music.
我喜欢看电影。
Wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng.
I like watching movies.
我喜欢看书。
Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū.
I like reading books.
我喜欢唱歌。
Wǒ xǐhuan chànggē.
I like singing.
