The Fighting Cricket

An honest man ruined by an emperor's demand for fighting crickets is saved by a miraculous cricket that wins every match. · HSK 3-4 · 9 sentences · pinyin + English

一人飞升，仙及鸡犬。信夫！
Yì rén fēishēng, xiān jí jī quǎn. Xìn fū!
When one man ascends to heaven, even his chickens and dogs rise with him. How true!
古代一位皇帝上好斗蟋蟀，便令天下家家供献。
Gǔdài yí wèi huángdì shàng hǎo dòu xīshuài, biàn lìng tiānxià jiājiā gòng xiàn.
An emperor of old loved cricket fights and ordered every family to supply one.
有个老实人叫成名，为找一只好蟋蟀，家境萧条。
Yǒu gè lǎoshí rén jiào Chéng Míng, wèi zhǎo yì zhī hǎo xīshuài, jiā jìng xiāotiáo.
An honest man named Cheng Ming, hunting for a good cricket, was driven to ruin.
他好不容易找到一只，却被儿子不小心放跑了。
Tā hǎobù róngyì zhǎodào yì zhī, què bèi érzi bù xiǎoxīn fàng pǎo le.
He finally found one, but his son carelessly let it escape.
成名急得大哭，不知如何交代。
Chéng Míng jí de dà kū, bù zhī rúhé jiāodài.
Cheng Ming wept in despair, not knowing how to answer for it.
绝望之中，忽见一只又大又勇猛的蟋蟀，从他脚下跳出。
Juéwàng zhī zhōng, hūjiàn yì zhī yòu dà yòu yǒngměng de xīshuài, cóng tā jiǎoxià tiào chū.
In his despair, a huge, fierce cricket suddenly jumped out from under his feet.
这只蟋蟀奇异无比，竟连战连捷。
Zhè zhī xīshuài qí yìng wú bǐ, jìng lián zhàn lián jié.
That cricket was extraordinary and won every single fight.
成名因它摆脱了苦难，全家都受到封赏。
Chéng Míng yīn tā bǎi tuō le kǔnàn, quán jiā dōu shòu dào fēngshǎng.
Thanks to it, Cheng Ming escaped his troubles and his whole family was rewarded.
蒲松龄借此讥讽：一个人得势，身边的人都跟着受益。
Pú sōnglíng jiè cǐ jī fěng: Yì gè rén dé shì, shēnbiān de rén dōu gēnzhe shòu yì.
Pu Songling mocks how, when one person gains power, everyone around him benefits.
