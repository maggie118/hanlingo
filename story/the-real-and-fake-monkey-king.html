The Real and Fake Monkey King

Two identical monkey kings fight each other, and no one in heaven or on earth can tell which is the real Sun Wukong. · HSK 1-2 · 3 sentences · pinyin + English

我是真的孙悟空！
Wǒ shì zhēn de Sūn Wùkōng!
I am the real Sun Wukong!
不，我才是真的！
Bù, wǒ cái shì zhēn de!
No, I am the real one!
菩萨，您能分辨出谁是真的吗？
Púsà, nín néng fēnbiàn chū shéi shì zhēn de ma?
Bodhisattva, can you tell who is the real one?
