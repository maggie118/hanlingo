Sun Tzu: Weakness and Strength

The skilled commander controls the battlefield by striking where the enemy is weak and never engaging where they are strong. · HSK 5-6 · 2 sentences · pinyin + English

好领导善于致人而不致于人，让团队跟着自己的节奏走，而不是被牵着鼻子走。
Hǎo lǐngdǎo shànyú zhì rén ér bú zhì yú rén, ràng tuánduì gēnzhe zìjǐ de jiézòu zǒu, ér bú shì bèi qiānzhe bízi zǒu.
A good leader moves others without being moved — leading the team to his rhythm instead of being led around.
竞争要懂得避实而击虚，不打价格战，而攻对手的薄弱环节。
Jìngzhēng yào dǒngde bì shí ér jī xū, bù dǎ jiàgé zhàn, ér gōng duìshǒu de bóruò huánjié.
In competition, strike where your rival is weak rather than in a price war.
