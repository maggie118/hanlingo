Life Philosophy & Long-term Vision

人生天地间，若白驹过隙，唯有内心充实方不虚此行。
Rén shēng tiān dì jiān, ruò bái jū guò xì, wéi yǒu nèi xīn chōng shí fāng bù xū cǐ xíng.
Human life between heaven and earth is a fleeting white horse; fulfillment prevents wasted trips.
真正的成熟，是看清生活的真相后依然热爱生活。
Zhēn zhèng de chéng shú, shì kàn qīng shēng huó de zhēn xiàng hòu yī rán rè ài shēng huó.
True maturity is loving life after seeing its truth clearly.
种一棵树最好的时间是十年前，其次是现在。

The best time to plant a tree was 10 years ago; the second best is now.

Huí shǒu guò wǎng de suì yuè, nà xiē céng jīng de kǎn kě dōu chéng le shēng mìng de xūn zhāng.
Looking back, past bumps all became life's medals.
