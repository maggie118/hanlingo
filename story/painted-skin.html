The Painted Skin

A scholar brings home a beautiful woman who turns out to be a demon wearing a painted skin. · HSK 3-4 · 9 sentences · pinyin + English

愚哉世人！明明妖也，而以为美。
Yú zāi shìrén! Míngmíng yāo yě, ér yǐwéi měi.
How foolish are mortals! Clearly a demon, yet taken for a beauty.
一个书生在路上遇到一位美貌女子，便带她回家。
Yí gè shūshēng zài lùshang yùdào yí wèi měimào nǚzǐ, biàn dài tā huí jiā.
A scholar met a beautiful woman on the road and brought her home.
一天，书生偷偷往她屋里一看，吓得魂飞魄散。
Yì tiān, shūshēng tōutōu wǎng tā wū lǐ yí kàn, xià dé huīfēipòsàn.
One day the scholar peeked into her room and was scared out of his wits.
床上躺着一个绿面恶鬼，正拿着一张人皮在画！
Chuáng shang tǎng zhe yí gè lǜ miàn è guǐ, zhèng ná zhe yì zhāng rénpí zài huà!
A green-faced demon lay on the bed, painting on a human skin!
原来那美女，不过是一张画了面的皮。
Yuánlái nà měinǚ, búguò shì yì zhāng huà le miàn de pí.
The beauty was nothing but a painted skin.
恶鬼发现被人看破，便挖走了书生的心。
È guǐ fāxiàn bèi rén kàn pò, biàn wā zǒu le shūshēng de xīn.
The demon, caught, tore out the scholar's heart.
书生的妻子哭着去求一位道士除妖。
Shūshēng de qīzi kū zhe qù qiú yí wèi dàoshi chú yāo.
His wife wept and begged a Taoist to destroy the demon.
道士用剑斩了恶鬼，又让妻子把心放回书生胸中。
Dàoshi yòng jiàn zhǎn le è guǐ, yòu ràng qīzi bǎ xīn fàng huí shūshēng xiōng zhōng.
The Taoist slew the demon with his sword and had the wife put the heart back.
书生居然复活了，一家人终于团聚。
Shūshēng jūrán fùhuó le, yì jiā rén zhōngyú tuánjù.
The scholar came back to life, and the family reunited.
