Sun Tzu: Waging War

War must be swift — a prolonged campaign exhausts the army and drains the state, so strike fast and end quickly. · HSK 5-6 · 1 sentence · pinyin + English

谈判讲究速战速决，兵贵胜，不贵久，拖得越久越被动。
Tánpàn jiǎngjiu sùzhàn sùjué, bīng guì shèng, bú guì jiǔ, tuō dé yuè jiǔ yuè bèidòng.
Negotiations should be quick — victory matters, not prolonging things; the longer you drag it out, the worse off you are.
