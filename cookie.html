1. What HanLingo uses

HanLingo is designed to be local-first. To remember your progress and preferences, it primarily uses your browser's local storage rather than tracking cookies. Local storage stays on your device and is not sent to us unless you opt into an account or sync feature.

2. Local storage we use

PurposeDataDuration
Learning progress & streakCompleted sentences, daily streakUntil you clear it
Vocabulary & settingsSaved words, preferencesUntil you clear it
Access / purchase stateWhether packs are unlockedUntil you clear it

You can review and clear this data at any time from the Progress panel ("Export JSON" / "Clear local data") or via your browser's site-data settings.

3. Third-party cookies (if applicable)

When you make a purchase, our payment provider may place its own cookies or equivalent technologies needed to process the payment. Any analytics or advertising services we add in the future will be listed here, with their purpose and how to opt out.

4. Managing cookies and local storage

You can control cookies and local storage through your browser settings (e.g. block third-party cookies, or clear site data for this domain). Note that clearing site data will remove your locally saved progress unless you export a backup first.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at this address with a revised date.
