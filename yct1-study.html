📝 YCT 1 Core Grammar 6 patterns

1. "是" (shì) — Subject + 是 + Noun

我是学生。
Wǒ shì xué sheng.
I am a student.
她是老师。
Tā shì lǎo shī.
She is a teacher.
这是书。
Zhè shì shū.
This is a book.

2. "有" (yǒu) — Subject + 有 + Object

我有朋友。
Wǒ yǒu péng you.
I have friends.
她有猫。
Tā yǒu māo.
She has a cat.
学校有很多学生。
Xué xiào yǒu hěn duō xué sheng.
The school has many students.

3. 正反疑问句 — Verb + 不 + Verb?

你好不好？
Nǐ hǎo bù hǎo?
Are you okay?
她是不是老师？
Tā shì bú shì lǎo shī?
Is she a teacher?
你去不去学校？
Nǐ qù bú qù xué xiào?
Are you going to school?

4. "在" (zài) — Subject + 在 + Place

我在学校。
Wǒ zài xué xiào.
I am at school.
书在桌子上。
Shū zài zhuō zi shàng.
The book is on the table.
她在家里。
Tā zài jiā lǐ.
She is at home.

5. 助动词 — 会 / 想 / 要

我会说中文。
Wǒ huì shuō Zhōng wén.
I can speak Chinese.
我想吃苹果。
Wǒ xiǎng chī píng guǒ.
I want to eat an apple.
我要喝水。
Wǒ yào hē shuǐ.
I want to drink water.

6. 疑问句 — 句尾 + 吗 (ma)

你好吗？
Nǐ hǎo ma?
How are you?
你是学生吗？
Nǐ shì xué sheng ma?
Are you a student?
你有朋友吗？
Nǐ yǒu péng you ma?
Do you have friends?
